## **Scene Progression**

The scenes will sometimes be location shifts, but at other times it will just be shifting conversation partners within a location. The map will be more about progressing through the story of Scripture than geographically oriented; it reveals itself as the verses are engaged with, either directly in the story (Daniel 1) or through conversation with the side characters about the cross references (AI-powered explorations). Progress gates will be defined based on progressing through the main text in discrete narrative chunks (Daniel 1 scenes). Nothing in the text can be skipped, but any and every side conversation (AI characters) could be skipped.

## **Cross Reference Character Vision**

I still need to curate the cross references and create characters out of them, but my instinct is that we will want different ones for different areas of the Bible (i.e., one for Genesis-Deuteronomy, one for OT historic books, one for OT Poetry, one for Prophets, one for Gospels/Acts, one for NT letters). The primary goal of the cross reference encounters is to help people see how these other texts illuminate the story of Daniel 1—the conversation will center on how it is relevant, and the player will receive game currency for getting the connection right.
