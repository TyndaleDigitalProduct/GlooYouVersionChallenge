## Story characters

### Daniel

- Belteshazzar. That's what they'll call me now. They can write whatever they
  want on the tablet. God knows my name.
- A name is what someone calls you. It isn't who you are.

### Hananiah

- Shadrach. I keep saying it and it still doesn't sound like me.
- My name meant "the LORD is gracious." I'm going to keep believing that,
  whatever they call me.

### Mishael

- Meshach now, apparently. My real name asks a question: "Who is like God?"
  No clay tablet changes the answer.
- They stamped it while I was still saying my old one.

### Azariah

- Abednego. They can hand me a new name. They can't hand me a new God.
- Say our real names when it's just us, all right? I don't want to forget the
  sound.

### Ashpenaz

- The scribe will read your new names. Learn to answer to them quickly; the
  king doesn't repeat himself.
- A Babylonian name is a door, boys. Every door in this palace opens faster
  for one.

## Encounter: GEN.41.39-45 (Torah (Gen-Deut))

- **Curated note (from refs.json, already final):** Joseph was another young Hebrew a foreign empire renamed: Pharaoh gave him an Egyptian name, an Egyptian wife, and sweeping power in Egypt.
