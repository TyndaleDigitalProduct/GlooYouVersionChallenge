## Setting

From `daniel-1.refs.json`: **Jerusalem under siege**

Dusk on the walls of Jerusalem. Watch-fires on the horizon mark the Babylonian
camp; inside the gates the streets are tense and quiet. Daniel and his three
friends, young nobles of the city, wait for news like everyone else.

## Lamplighter

### Opening (presents the full passage, DAN.1.1)

- There you are. Stay close to the lamp; the city is dark tonight, and
  something darker than night is on its way.
- This is where it all begins. See for yourself. I'll hold the light.

  **[SCRIPTURE CARD: DAN.1.1 opens here. The scene passage, read before free
  movement begins.]**

- Walk around, talk to people. A few of them carry lanterns; they've been
  reading this story longer than anyone in the city, and what they know is
  worth hearing.

### Exit — all encounters engaged

- You listened to them all, the record and the warning both. So you already
  know the strangest thing about this siege: it didn't catch God by surprise.
  Come on. Let's see what this night brings.

### Exit — some encounters engaged

- You heard some of what this city has to say. There's always more, but no
  lamp burns forever. Come on. Let's see what this night brings.

### Exit — no encounters engaged

- In a hurry? I understand; sieges do that to people. The lantern-carriers
  will keep their stories for another time. Come on. Let's see what this
  night brings.

## Story characters

### Daniel

- The watchmen say the army stretches past the horizon. Whatever happens
  next, God isn't finished with us.
- My father named me "God is my judge." Not Babylon. I'm holding on to that
  tonight.

### Hananiah

- My mother says we should pray. So I'm praying.
- Do you hear how quiet the streets are? A whole city holding its breath.

### Mishael

- I counted the watch-fires from the wall until I lost count.
- The elders keep arguing about what Egypt will do. Egypt feels very far away
  tonight.

### Azariah

- If the city falls, do you think God falls with it? Because I don't.
- I keep thinking about the prophets. Everyone said they were too gloomy.

### Nebuchadnezzar

- Egypt is not coming to save this city. No one is coming to save this city.
- When the gates open, bring me the best of it: the treasures and the sons.
  Babylon wastes nothing.

## NPCs

- **Gatekeeper**: Forty years I've kept this gate. Never barred it against
  anything like this.
- **A mother**: Inside, both of you, right now. The street is no place for
  children tonight.
- **Soldier on the wall**: Stay off the wall. The watch changes at dusk.
- **Market vendor**: No bread today. Maybe none tomorrow, either.
