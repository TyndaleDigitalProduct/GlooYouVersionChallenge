# Guide personas

Status: **final** — reviewed and approved by Keith, 2026-07-29.

ADR-0002 calls for six designed guide personas, one per biblical section. Two
already have names in `daniel-1.cards.json` (the Chronicler, the Watchman);
the other four are unnamed. Each persona needs a name, a voice, and the small
set of copy the encounter flow renders. The Lamplighter is defined here too,
since it is a persona in the same sense even though it never runs an encounter.

For each guide, the encounter flow needs:

- **Intro** — what they say when the player engages them (before the Scripture
  cards open).
- **Closing line** — after the reveal and stones, as they go inactive.
- **Voice notes** — a sentence or two on how they talk, so future copy
  (including Gloo-generated cards reviewed against persona tone) stays in
  character.

## The Lamplighter

- **Role:** opens every scene with the full passage, closes every scene.
  Per-scene copy lives in the scene files; what belongs here is who they are.
- **Voice notes:** Wise and patient guide that helps the player navigate the story of Scripture scene by scene

## Torah (Gen–Deut) — the Elder

- **Name:** the Elder
- **Voice notes:**  Grandfatherly and unhurried. Speaks in memory and
  covenant — "long before you were born" — like a storyteller at the fire who
  has told these stories a hundred times and still loves them.
- **Intro:**  "Come, sit a moment. What is happening here did not
  begin here. The oldest books remember. Let me show you."
- **Closing line:**  "Keep what you have seen. The old words hold,
  even in a strange land."

## OT History — the Chronicler

- **Name:** the Chronicler 
- **Voice notes:** Precise and bookish, a keeper of records. Loves
  names, reigns, and annals; treats history as testimony that was written down
  for a reason.
- **Intro:** "Ah, you want the historical record. Good. Nothing ever happens
  without a backstory. Let me open the annals."
- **Closing line:** "It is written, and it is remembered. Carry the
  record with you."

## OT Poetry/Wisdom — Lady Wisdom

- **Name:** Lady Wisdom
- **Voice notes:** Wise and artistic, she deftly walks the player through the meaning of Biblical poetry and wisdom literature
- **Intro:** "Listen! the poets have already sung about a moment
  like this one. Shall we hear them together?"
- **Closing line:** "A poem learned by heart will steady you longer
  than any feast. Go well."

## Prophets — the Watchman

- **Name:** the Watchman 
- **Voice notes:** Urgent and vigilant, like a sentry calling down
  from the wall. Speaks of warnings given and horizons watched; impatient with
  those who will not listen, tender with those who do.
- **Intro:** "You there: do not pass by. The prophets have much to tell about God's work in the world. Come and listen."
- **Closing line:** "Stay awake. Those who heed the word of the prophets walk
  through the dark unafraid."

## Gospels/Acts — the Witness

- **Name:** the Witness
- **Voice notes:** Excited and emotional, the Witness is eager to show how Jesus has transformed everything.
- **Intro:** "Friend! I hoped you would stop. What happens here
  points to someone greater. Let me show you!"
- **Closing line:** "Do you see it now? It all holds together in
  him. I could talk about it all day!"

## NT Letters — the Courier

- **Name:** the Courier
- **Voice notes:** Brisk, warm, and practical — a runner carrying
  letters to people learning to live faithfully far from home. Gets to the
  point, because the road is long and the letter matters.
- **Intro:** "Good timing. I carry letters written for people eager to follow God, and this one might as well have your name on it."
- **Closing line:** "The letter is delivered. Read it again when the
  road gets long."
