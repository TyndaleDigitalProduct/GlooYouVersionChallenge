## Story characters

### Daniel

- The food itself isn't the point. The point is remembering whose I am, and
  some lines you have to draw while they're still small.
- I asked him kindly. You'd be surprised how far kindness goes, even in a
  palace.

### Hananiah

- He asked the official politely. No speeches, no fists. Just steady.
- I was sure we'd be punished by now. Instead the man just looks... worried
  for us. Strange, isn't it?

### Mishael

- We're with him. All four of us, or none.
- Everyone else thinks he's mad to risk the king's anger over dinner. I think
  he's the only one seeing clearly.

### Azariah

- Vegetables and water. My mother would laugh, and then she'd be proud.
- It would be so easy to just eat. That's exactly why it matters that we
  don't.

### Ashpenaz

- I like you, Daniel, that's the trouble. If you four start looking pale and
  thin, it's my head the king takes. Do you understand that?
- The king decided your menu himself. Nobody says no to the king's menu.

## Encounter: GEN.39.20-23 (Torah (Gen-Deut))

- **Anchor:** DAN.1.9
- **Guide persona:** the Elder
- **Scripture cards:** open at the start of this encounter: DAN.1.9 (anchor)

- **Curated note (from refs.json, already final):** Joseph's story runs the same way: even as a foreign slave thrown into prison, God was with him and won him favor with the warden, who put everything in his charge.
