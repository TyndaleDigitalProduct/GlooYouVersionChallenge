## Story characters

### Daniel

- I understood a dream last night. Really understood it, the way you
  understand a friend's voice. That doesn't come from tablets. God gave it.
- The more they teach us, the more I pray. Those two things are connected.

### Hananiah

- Funny thing about Babylon's books: the more of them I read, the bigger God
  looks standing next to them.
- I answered a question today that our tutor couldn't. He just stared at me.

### Mishael

- The tutors come to us with questions now. It's the strangest thing.
- I actually like the astronomy. The stars belong to God anyway; Babylon
  just catalogued them.

### Azariah

- Study hard, pray harder. That's the whole curriculum, if you ask me.
- Three years ago I couldn't read a word of this language. Tell me that's not
  a gift.

## NPCs

- **Chaldean tutor**: Twenty years teaching the king's trainees. I've never
  had to prepare this hard for my own students' questions.
- **Library attendant**: Tablets back on the right shelf, please. The catalog
  took a lifetime.
- **Student from another province**: I study all night and still place behind
  the Judeans. All four of them.

## Encounter: GEN.41.15-16 (Torah (Gen-Deut))

- **Anchor:** DAN.1.17
- **Guide persona:** the Elder
- **Scripture cards:** open at the start of this encounter: DAN.1.17 (anchor)
  and GEN.41.15-16; both must be read before the six insight cards unlock.
- **Curated note (from refs.json, already final):** Joseph is the Bible's other great example of a Hebrew in a foreign court with a God-given gift for dreams. Asked to interpret Pharaoh's dream, he is quick to say the ability is not his own; God will give the answer. That is exactly the gift this verse credits to Daniel, and the same humble source.
