## Story characters

### Daniel

- Three years of training for one conversation. God was preparing us the
  whole time; the king only scheduled the meeting.
- Whatever he asks, we answer honestly. Honest has worked so far.

### Hananiah

- The king asked me about law and literature, and the answers were just...
  there. Given.
- I bowed to the king. My heart stayed standing; it belongs somewhere else.

### Mishael

- All that gold, and the guards, and the throne, and my knees held. God held
  them.
- Did you hear? None of the others came close. He kept all four of us.

### Azariah

- We walked in as captives. We walked out as the king's own advisors. Try
  explaining that without God.
- Somewhere in Jerusalem there's a burned gate with my family's name near it.
  And now I stand here. What a strange road.

### Nebuchadnezzar

- I have interviewed every scholar this program produced. Where have these
  four been?
- Judah's God interests me. Judah's young men interest me more. They stay.
