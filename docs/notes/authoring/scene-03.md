## Setting

From `daniel-1.refs.json`: **Babylon palace; selection & training program**

A colonnaded courtyard in Nebuchadnezzar's palace. Young men from Judah stand
in nervous rows while officials with tablets walk the lines, choosing the best
for three years of training at the king's expense, and the king's table.

## Story characters

### Daniel

- They chose us for their program. God chose us first. I intend to remember
  the order.
- Three years to learn everything Babylon knows. Fine. Let's see what they
  make of what we already know.

### Hananiah

- We can learn their words without swallowing their gods. I think. I hope.
- Did you see the size of this place? Our whole street would fit in this
  courtyard.

### Mishael

- The library has ten thousand tablets, someone said. Ten thousand!
- They keep calling us "the ones without defect." I feel plenty defective
  this morning.

### Azariah

- The food smells incredible. Honestly, that worries me more than the books
  do.
- Three years. That's a long time to remember who you are, every single day.

### Ashpenaz

- Stand up straight. The king asked for young men with no flaws, quick in
  every kind of learning, and I intend to give him exactly that.
- Three years, all paid for, eating from the king's own kitchen. There are
  princes in this city who'd envy what you're being handed.

## Encounter: ACT.7.20-22 (Gospels/Acts)

- **Anchor:** DAN.1.4
- **Guide persona:** the Witness
- **Scripture cards:** open at the start of this encounter: DAN.1.4 (anchor)
  and ACT.7.20-22; both must be read before the six insight cards unlock.
- **Curated note (from refs.json, already final):** A close parallel to Daniel: like Daniel, Moses was a Hebrew raised inside a foreign empire's court and trained in all its wisdom (Egypt's, in his case). Neither let the empire's education erase who they were; both stayed faithful and were used by God to rescue his people. It is the same challenge Daniel faces here, mastering Babylon's learning without losing his faith.
