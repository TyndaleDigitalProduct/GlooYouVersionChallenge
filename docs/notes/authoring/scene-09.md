## Story characters

### Daniel

- Kings come and go. I've decided to stop being surprised by that.
- Ten times better, they say. I keep telling them the arithmetic isn't mine;
  wisdom belongs to God, and he's generous with it.

### Hananiah

- They put our names in the court records today. Babylon writes us down; God
  wrote us down first.
- Remember the night the city fell? I was sure the story was over. It was
  barely the first page.

### Mishael

- The magicians finally asked Daniel his secret. He told them. They didn't
  believe it could be that simple.
- We answer to the king now, and the king answers to God, whether he knows
  it yet or not.

### Azariah

- Seventy years, Jeremiah said. We'll still be here, planted. Watch.
- Vegetables and water, and now this table. I laugh about it sometimes.

## NPCs

- **Court magician**: We chart the stars all night. He asks his God and
  sleeps fine. I can't argue with the results.
- **Palace messenger**: Message for Belteshazzar: the king's asking for him.
  Again.
- **Junior scribe**: They say he'll outlast us all. The way things are going,
  I believe it.
